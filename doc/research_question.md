# Smalltalk Museum Robot - Research Question

## Context

Previous research has resulted in the development of a museum robot.
The robot is capable of providing guided tours to museum visitors.  It
autonomously:

1. Offers guided tours to individual visitors
2. Recognises consent for such a guided tour
3. Leads the visitor to each exhibit in turn
4. Interprets visitor queries concerning each exhibit
5. Provides a verbal description of the current exhibit

Research studies can build on this platform by having museum visitors
experience a guided tour from the robot, collecting their evaluations,
and using these to guide the development of improvements to the
system.

## Research Question

Does adjusting robot behaviour based on small talk during the journey
between exhibits enhance the robot acceptance of the visitor and lead
to a more enjoyable visit, compared to purely functional interaction
during those journeys?

## Constructs

[Small Talk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_talk)
: A conversational style involving the exchange of short questions and
  answers concerning topics of relatively low emotional or cognitive
  load.

Robot Acceptance
: The degree to which a human interacting with a robot accepts the
  presence of the robot as a natural part of their environment, rather
  than a source of stress or discomfort.

Visit Enjoyment
: The extent to which a visitor to a museum derives both short term
  enjoyment while visiting, as well as the subsequent benefit the
  visitor receives from having visited the museum in the past.
