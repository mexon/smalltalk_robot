
# SIMULATION OF DIALOGUE
The dialogue in here based on 2 type criteria of question: Alvin and Matthew

I will answer only 2 questions here for each criteria, so in total there will be 4 questions i answered. Each question will be answered with 2 personas described, interested in robot and not interested in robot.

DISCLAIMER:
The answer is just the simulation or estimation of how each persona will answer. I will provide the answer in straight way, which without sarcasm or hidden meaning answers. 



Symbol:
1. I use CAPITAL words to express emotion like angry caused by uncomfortable situation. So in real life, maybe it sound like the volume of the voice is increased
2. "..." means the dialogue continue
3. "-" means uncomfortable
4. A. means the responder of interested persona, while B. means the responder of not interested persona
5. "-->" means note or conclusion

Simple RULES:
1. If the B answering question with:
"I prefer you to be quite" OR "I prefer you to only explain the exhibit" OR similar kind of answer, then the robot must be automatically turn off the small talk. 

2. If 3 questions that could be answered in long answer, answered in short form, then the small talk will not always turned on. It will be 50% chance it turned on. 

3. If 5 questions that could be answered in long answer, answered in short form, then the small talk will be turned off. 

4. If for example, the guest answer a question in long form after several question answered in short form, then the count will be reseted to 0.
