# Museum Visitor Experience: Proposed Capability Survey

We are exploring a possible new capability at the museum and would like to understand visitors’ needs and initial reactions. No decision has been made about introducing it.

You do not need specialist knowledge to participate. Positive, negative and uncertain responses are equally useful. Please answer based on your own interests and needs.

Part 1: Your museum visits

1. How often do you visit museums?
Select one.

* This would be my first museum visit
* Less than once a year
* Once or twice a year
* Several times a year
* Monthly or more often

2. Who do you usually visit museums with?
Select all that apply.

* I visit on my own
* A partner or other adults
* Children

* An organised group
* Other: ______
* It varies

3. What do you usually hope to get from a museum visit?
Select up to three.

* See and appreciate objects or artworks

* Explore something unfamiliar
* Reflect on personal experiences or meaningful topics
* Spend time with others
* Support someone else’s interests or learning
* Relax or enjoy the surroundings
